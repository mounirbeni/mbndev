## Top 3 Channels to Focus On (in order)

### 🥇 1. Cold Instagram/WhatsApp DMs to Moroccan businesses (Days 1–3)

**Why:** Morocco runs on Instagram. Riads, restaurants, boutiques, tour guides all post there daily — but 70%+ have no real website, just a Linktree or bio link. You've already built projects in this exact niche.

**Who to target:**
- Riads in Marrakech, Fes, Chefchaouen with Instagram but no proper website
- Tour operators / experience guides (desert trips, cooking classes, etc.)
- Small e-commerce brands selling Moroccan products (argan, ceramics, fashion)
- Moroccan clinics, beauty salons, pharmacies with no site

**How to find them:**
1. Search Instagram hashtags: `#riadmarrakech`, `#boutiquemarocaine`, `#madeInMorocco`, `#guidemarrakech`, `#ecommerceMaroc`
2. Look at their bio — if they have no website link or just a WhatsApp number → perfect target
3. Check if their Google Business listing has "Website: —" = no site

**DM Template (French — most Moroccan businesses respond to French):**

```
Bonjour [Prénom] 👋

J'ai vu votre page — vos [produits/photos/riad] sont vraiment impressionnants. 

Je suis Mounir, développeur web basé au Maroc. Je crée des sites professionnels pour les entreprises comme la vôtre — rapides, modernes, et qui convertissent les visiteurs en clients.

J'ai récemment travaillé sur des projets similaires : [mention 1 relevant project, e.g., RiadConnect pour les riads, TyyMaroc pour le e-commerce].

Je serais heureux de vous proposer quelque chose d'adapté à votre budget. Un appel de 15 min cette semaine ?

→ mbndev.ma
```

**WhatsApp Template (shorter, more casual):**

```
Salam [Prénom] 👋 j'ai vu votre Instagram — super concept. Je suis développeur web et je crée des sites pro pour les business marocains. Est-ce que vous avez un site web pour votre activité en ce moment ?
```

*(If they say no → "Je peux vous faire quelque chose de rapide et pro à partir de 800$ — envie qu'on en parle ?")*

### 🥈 2. Upwork Profile + Targeted Proposals (Days 2–5)

**Why:** International clients, immediate access to people already paying for dev work, no cold discovery needed.

**Profile setup (do this once):**

- Headline: `Next.js & Full-Stack Developer | Websites, E-Commerce & SaaS for Growing Businesses`
- Overview: Lead with your 5 live projects and link them. Mention Morocco + remote worldwide. Mention you deliver with a client dashboard for full transparency.
- Add all 5 portfolio projects with screenshots and the live links.
- Hourly rate: start at $25–35/hr to get first review, raise after.

**Proposal Template (adapt per job):**

```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you need [specific thing from their post] — I've built exactly this type of project recently.

Here's proof: [link to most relevant demo project]. Same stack, same requirements.

What I'd do for you:
- [3 bullet points specific to their job post]

Timeline: [X days]. I work via a client dashboard where you can track every update in real time.

Want to jump on a quick call? I can usually start within 48h.

— Mounir | mbndev.ma
```

**What jobs to apply to:**
- "Build a landing page" → Starter package, fast win
- "E-commerce website" → show TyyMaroc or LueurSkin
- "Riad/hotel website" → show RiadConnect
- "Web app / dashboard" → show CarryLink
- Budget: target $500–$2000 jobs, avoid $50–$100 gigs

**Key tip:** Apply within the first hour of a job being posted. Early applicants win.

### 🥉 3. LinkedIn Outreach to Startup Founders (Days 4–7)

**Why:** Moroccan and MENA-region founders are on LinkedIn. They have budget, they need professional web presence, and they respond to DMs.

**Who to target:**
- Founders listed on moroccanstartups.com, startupblink.com, nextwave.ma
- Company pages with no website or a bad one
- Founders who recently posted about "launching" something

**LinkedIn Connection Request Note:**

```
Hi [Name] — saw you're building [company]. I'm a full-stack dev based in Morocco (mbndev.ma). I specialize in web products for Moroccan startups. Would love to connect and maybe help if you ever need a dev.
```

**After they accept (Day 2 follow-up):**

```
Thanks for connecting! Quick question — are you happy with your current web presence, or is that something still on the to-do list? I ask because I've built a few products for Moroccan startups recently and always like to see if there's a fit.
```

## Follow-up Rule (Most people ignore this)

80% of replies come from follow-ups, not first messages. If someone doesn't reply in 3 days:

```
Hey [Name], just checking in on my last message. Still worth a quick chat?
```

That's it. Short and human. Do it.
